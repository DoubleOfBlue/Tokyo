Tokyo Transit Planner

Pin spots for your trip and get real JR / Metro / Toei transit directions between any two pins — no credit card needed.

This app uses only what the free Maps Demo Key supports — map rendering and place search — so no billing account is required. Get one at developers.google.com/maps/demo-key (click “Get a Demo Key,” sign in with any Google account, no card). A standard billed key also works if you have one.
